Features

Print it big. Keep it sharp.

Print Studio turns any photo, poster or drawing into a print file: A4 to A2, Letter, Tabloid, 4×6 to 24×36 in, 60×90 cm or your own size, at 150, 240 or 300 dpi. Grandio computes the enlargement, rates the quality (Excellent, Good or Limit) before you print, and lets you drag the crop or add margins.

FAQ

Is Grandio free?

Yes for ×2: enlarge any number of images ×2 in Auto mode and export them in JPEG or PNG, with no watermark, no quota and no account. Grandio Pro, a one-time purchase, unlocks Print Studio, the Etsy print pack, Old Photo mode and denoising, ×4 and custom sizes, batches, HEIC and TIFF.

Will my print be sharp?

Print Studio tells you before you print. It computes the enlargement the print needs: up to 2× is rated Excellent, up to 4× Good, beyond Limit. Lower the size or the resolution (240 or 150 dpi suit posters seen from a distance) to improve the rating.

Are my photos sent anywhere?

No. The enhancement runs on your Mac with models included in the app. Grandio has no network access: your photos never leave your Mac. See the privacy policy.

How does it work?

Grandio uses Real-ESRGAN neural networks (by Xintao Wang, BSD licence), converted to Core ML. They were measured against classic resizing on photos, old prints, drawings and screenshots before being chosen.

Does it restore faces?

Not specifically: Grandio sharpens and cleans the whole image. It has no dedicated face model, and it does not invent or change people's features on purpose.

How big can the result be?

Up to 16,384 pixels per side and 256 megapixels. Grandio tells you before starting if a size is too large.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. Grandio runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It is fastest on Apple silicon, where it uses the Neural Engine.

Can I use Pro on several Macs, or share it with my family?

Yes. Grandio Pro works on every Mac signed in with your Apple Account (Grandio › Restore Purchases), and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
