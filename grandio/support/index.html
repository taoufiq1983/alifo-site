Getting started

Enlarge a photo
Drop a photo on the window (or click Choose Photos…, or paste with ⌘V). The result appears in the comparator: drag the divider, zoom with a pinch or ⌘ + scroll, press Space to see the original. Click Export (⌘E).
Try it without your own photos
Click Try a Sample: four public-domain images open (an old photo, a small photo, a drawing and a screenshot).
A whole folder
Drop a folder (Grandio Pro). Every image is listed; Export All, at the bottom of the list, writes them into the folder you choose.
From the Finder
Select images, right-click › Services › Upscale with Grandio. If the item is missing, enable it in System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › Services.

Common questions

“The result would be … pixels”
Grandio creates images up to 16,384 pixels per side and 256 megapixels. Choose ×2, or a smaller custom size.
The export does not replace my file
On purpose: Grandio never overwrites. It names the result “photo@2x.jpg”, “photo@4x.jpg”, and adds a number if that name exists.
“Grandio cannot save in …”
The folder is read-only or on a locked disk. Choose another folder.
Faces look different
Grandio has no face-specific model; on very small faces the AI may smooth details. Try ×2, Photo mode, or a lower denoise.
Restore my purchase
Grandio › Restore Purchases, or Settings › Grandio Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account used to buy.
Refunds
Purchases are made through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
