Privacy Policy

Grandio collects nothing. Effective November 2, 2026.

The short version

No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Your photos are enhanced on your Mac and never leave it.

Details

Your photos
Grandio opens only the images you choose, drop, paste or send from the Finder. The enhancement runs on your Mac with AI models included in the app. Images and results are never uploaded anywhere.
What stays on your Mac
Your exports are saved where you choose. Grandio keeps only its settings and whether Grandio Pro is unlocked. Images are not kept after you quit.
Metadata
Exports keep your photos’ metadata (date, camera, copyright) unless you turn it off; an option removes the location (GPS). This happens on your Mac.
Network
Grandio does not connect to the internet. It has no permission to do so (App Sandbox, no network access).
Data we collect
None. Grandio does not collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
Purchases
Grandio Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details.
Tracking
Grandio does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party code.
Children
Grandio suits all ages. No personal data, from a child or an adult, is collected.
Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
