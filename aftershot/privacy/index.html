Privacy Policy

AfterShot for iOS  ·  Effective August 12, 2026

AfterShot collects no data. There is no server behind the app, no account to create, and no analytics of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

AfterShot keeps your entries — visits, injections, check-ins, measurements, notes, and photos — in a local database and local files inside the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is transmitted. The app makes no network connections at all.

Camera

AfterShot asks for camera access for one purpose: taking photos of an injection site to attach to a check-in. Photos are saved inside the app's local storage only. They are not uploaded, not added to your photo library, and not shared with anyone unless you export them yourself.

Notifications

Check-in reminders are local notifications, scheduled entirely on your device. No notification service outside your iPhone is involved.

When data leaves your device

Data leaves your device only when you choose to export it — as a PDF report or a CSV file — and share it through the iOS share sheet. From that point, the copy you shared is governed by whatever destination you chose (mail, messages, files, print).

One more path exists and is under your control: your iPhone's own backup. AfterShot's storage is included in the device backup you configure in iOS settings (iCloud or computer backup). Apple's backup, not AfterShot, handles that copy.

What AfterShot never does

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no personal identifiers.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs that transmit data.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no sale or sharing of data — there is no data to sell.
  • No interpretation of your entries. AfterShot records what you enter; it does not evaluate it.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry, or everything at once, from inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device. Copies you exported earlier, and copies inside your own iPhone backups, remain under your control.

Children

AfterShot is intended for adults and for caregivers logging on behalf of a patient. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of AfterShot changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date. The current promise — no collection, no server — is the foundation of the product.
