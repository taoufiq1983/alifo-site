AfterShot

A private diary for allergy shot check-ins — for the hours and days after you leave the office.

Allergy immunotherapy means regular injections for years. What happens at the clinic is written down. What happens two hours later, or the next morning, usually isn't — and it's exactly what your allergist asks about at the next visit.

How it works

  • Log the shot as you leave. One tap per injection — a visit can carry one, two, or three.
  • Two quick check-ins. A 20-second follow-up at two hours and the next morning, with a reminder for each.
  • A one-page report. Export a clear PDF of your history to bring to your next appointment.

What AfterShot never does

  • It never sends your data anywhere. There is no server, no account, no analytics. Everything lives on your iPhone.
  • It never interprets your entries. AfterShot records what you observed — reading it is between you and your allergist.
  • It never locks your history. Your full history and PDF/CSV export are free, for life.
