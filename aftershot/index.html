What AfterShot never does.

The promise is structural, not a setting. There is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing collecting.

Never sends your data

No server, no account, no analytics. The app makes no network connections at all — everything lives on your iPhone.

Never interprets

AfterShot records what you observed. Reading it is between you and your allergist.

Never locks your history

Your full history and PDF/CSV export are free, for life. No subscription standing between you and your own diary.

Questions.

Is it really free?
Yes. Logging, check-ins, full history, and export are free forever. A future version may add optional extras — the diary itself will never be one of them.
Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone, and nowhere else. The details are in the privacy policy — it is short, because there is little to say when nothing is collected.
I missed a check-in. Is it lost?
No. Pending check-ins stay reachable from the Today screen and from the visit itself, whenever you get to them.
Does it work for children's treatment?
A parent or caregiver can keep the diary on their own iPhone on behalf of a patient.
