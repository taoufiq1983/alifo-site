Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone, and nowhere else. AfterShot has no server and makes no network connections. See the privacy policy.
I missed a check-in. Can I still answer it?
Yes. Pending check-ins stay reachable from the Today screen and from the visit itself, at any time.
Can I log a visit from a few days ago?
Yes. You can add a past visit with one to three injections. Dates you don't know are simply left unrecorded — AfterShot never fills one in for you.
How do I share my history with my allergist?
Open your history and export a one-page PDF, or a CSV if your clinic prefers raw data. Both are free, for your full history, always.
How do I delete my data?
Each entry can be deleted in the app, or you can erase everything from Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
