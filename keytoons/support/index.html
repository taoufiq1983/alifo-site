Common questions

I don't hear anything when I type.
Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring and turn KeyToons on. Then open the KeyToons panel from the menu bar and check that "Enable" is on and the volume is above zero.
Do you record what I type?
Never. KeyToons only detects that a key was pressed in order to play a sound. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
I bought the Premium Theme Pack on another Mac.
Open the KeyToons panel and click "Restore Purchases". Your themes unlock right away.
Can I preview a premium theme before buying?
Yes. Select any premium theme in the panel to hear a short preview.
How do I uninstall KeyToons?
Quit KeyToons from its panel, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
