Privacy Policy

KeyToons collects nothing. Effective October 26, 2026.

The short version

KeyToons has no server, no account and no analytics. It makes no network connections. What you type never leaves your Mac — in fact, KeyToons never reads it at all.

Details

Input Monitoring permission
macOS asks you to allow Input Monitoring so KeyToons can know when a key is pressed. KeyToons uses this only to play a sound at that moment. The content of your keystrokes is never recorded, stored, logged or transmitted.
Data we collect
None. KeyToons does not collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
Settings
Your volume, theme and on/off preferences are saved on your Mac only, and are removed when you delete the app.
Purchases
The Premium Theme Pack is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details.
Tracking
KeyToons does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party code that does.
Children
KeyToons collects no data from anyone, including children.
Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
