KeyToons

Fun keyboard sounds for your Mac. Every key has its own voice.

KeyToons lives quietly in your menu bar and plays a playful sound each time you press a key. Type an email and hear a melody. Hit Return and trigger an explosion. Delete a typo and hear a sad little duck.

Private by design

KeyToons only detects that a key was pressed in order to play a sound. What you type is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. No account, no tracking, no network connections. Read the privacy policy.
