Common questions

Which files does Extracto read?
PDF, PNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and WebP. PDFs that already contain text are read directly; scans and photos go through text recognition on your Mac.
"This PDF is protected by a password."
Open the PDF in Preview, enter its password, choose File › Export as PDF without a password, then drop the new file on Extracto.
Some text is wrong or missing.
Recognition works best on sharp, well-lit pages. For photos, hold the phone parallel to the page and avoid shadows; for scans, 200–300 dpi is ideal. Handwriting is not supported.
A table came out as plain text.
Extracto detects tables from aligned columns. Very irregular tables, or tables with merged cells, may come out as text: send us an example (without personal data) and we will look at it.
What is free, and what is Extracto Pro?
Text is free. Tables are Pro. Reading text is free with no limit: any number of pages, batches and folders, Copy All, and export to TXT, Markdown, Word and JSON. Extracto Pro, a one-time purchase, unlocks tables: the Tables tab, export to Excel and CSV, and real tables in Word and JSON. Without it, you see the first rows of each table, and the rest of the document stays free.
How can I try it without my own files?
Click "Try a sample" in the empty window (or Help › Try a Sample): Extracto opens three sample documents, including an invoice with a table.
I bought Extracto Pro on another Mac.
Open Extracto › Settings › Extracto Pro and click "Restore Purchases". The purchase is also shared with your family.
Where are my exports?
Wherever you chose to save them. Extracto never overwrites a file: duplicates get " 2", " 3"…
How do I uninstall Extracto?
Quit Extracto, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
