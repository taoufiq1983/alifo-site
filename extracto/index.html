FAQ

Is Extracto free?

Yes for text: read any number of pages, files and folders, copy everything, and export the text to Word, Markdown, plain text and JSON, with no limit and no account. Extracto Pro, a one-time purchase, unlocks tables.

Are my documents sent anywhere?

No. Text recognition runs on your Mac with Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and PDFKit). Extracto has no network access: your documents, their text and their tables never leave your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Which languages does it read?

17: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Thai, Korean, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). A scan or a photo in a script it can't read yet, such as Hebrew, Greek or Hindi, shows a clear message instead of wrong text.

How does it handle tables?

It finds rows and columns in PDFs, scans and photos, with or without ruling lines, and keeps numbers as numbers in Excel. Without Pro you see each table's header and first three rows. Very irregular tables, or merged cells, may come out as text.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. Extracto runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Which files can I open?

PDF, PNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF and WebP. Drop them, choose them or paste them with ⌘V. A password-protected PDF needs to be unlocked in Preview first.

Can I use Pro on several Macs, or share it with my family?

Yes. Extracto Pro works on every Mac signed in with your Apple Account (Settings › Extracto Pro › Restore Purchases), and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
