Privacy Policy

Extracto collects nothing. Effective October 31, 2026.

The short version

No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Your documents are read on your Mac and never leave it.

Details

Your documents
Extracto opens only the files you choose, drop or paste. Text recognition runs on your Mac with Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and PDFKit). Documents, extracted text and tables are never uploaded anywhere.
What stays on your Mac
Your exports are saved where you choose. Extracto keeps only its settings and the count of free pages used today. Results are not kept after you quit.
Network
Extracto does not connect to the internet. It has no permission to do so (App Sandbox, no network access).
Data we collect
None. Extracto does not collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
Purchases
Extracto Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details.
Tracking
Extracto does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party code.
Children
Extracto suits all ages. No personal data, from a child or an adult, is collected.
Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
