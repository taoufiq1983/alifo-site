Common questions

Alwah does not hear me.
Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn Alwah on. Recite clearly, close to the Mac, in a quiet room. Recite › "Test the microphone" checks the level.
Is my voice sent anywhere?
Never. Recognition runs on your Mac. Your last recitation stays on your Mac so you can listen to it again; you can delete it from the end-of-recitation screen. See the privacy policy.
Which surahs are free with the AI?
Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas. Reading and listening are free for the whole Quran.
I bought Alwah AI on another Mac.
Open Settings › Purchase and click "Restore Purchases". Alwah AI unlocks right away. The purchase is also shared with your family.
The reciter does not play.
The recitations come from the King Fahd Complex's servers: check your internet connection, then press Listen again.
How do I uninstall Alwah?
Quit Alwah, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
