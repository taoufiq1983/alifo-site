Alwah Support

Privacy Policy

Alwah collects nothing. Effective October 29, 2026.

The short version

No account, no ads, no tracking. Speech recognition runs on your Mac: your voice is never sent anywhere.

Details

What stays on your Mac
Your progress, your recitation history and your last recorded recitation stay on your Mac. You can delete your voice from the end-of-recitation screen.
The microphone
The microphone is used only while you recite. Speech recognition runs on your Mac: the recording is never sent anywhere.
What is downloaded
The text of the Quran is built into the app. The recitations (King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex), the tajweed colours (alquran.cloud) and the translations of the meanings (QuranEnc) are downloaded from their public sources when you use them. These downloads carry no personal data.
Data we collect
None. Alwah does not collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
Purchases
Alwah AI is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, email or payment details.
Tracking
Alwah does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party code that does.
Children
Alwah suits children and adults alike. No personal data, from a child or an adult, is collected.
Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
