Alwah

Learn the Quran by heart, the msid way. Recite out loud: the AI follows every word, right on your Mac.

Alwah follows the method taught for centuries in the Moroccan msid: listen to the board (lawh), read it, recite it, then wash it once the passage is known. Every day, your session brings back what you learnt recently, what is new, and what you learnt long ago.
