FAQ

Is Restora free?

Yes for ×2: enlarge any number of photos ×2 in Photo mode and export them in JPEG or PNG, with no limit and no account. Restora Pro, a one-time purchase, unlocks ×4 and custom sizes, batches, the Old Photo, Drawing and Screenshot modes, denoising, HEIC and TIFF.

Are my photos sent anywhere?

No. The enhancement runs on your Mac with models included in the app. Restora has no network access: your photos never leave your Mac. See the privacy policy.

How does it work?

Restora uses Real-ESRGAN neural networks (by Xintao Wang, BSD licence), converted to Core ML. They were measured against classic resizing on photos, old prints, drawings and screenshots before being chosen.

Does it restore faces?

Not specifically: Restora sharpens and cleans the whole image. It has no dedicated face model, and it does not invent or change people's features on purpose.

How big can the result be?

Up to 16,384 pixels per side and 256 megapixels. Restora tells you before starting if a size is too large.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. Restora runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It is fastest on Apple silicon, where it uses the Neural Engine.

Can I use Pro on several Macs, or share it with my family?

Yes. Restora Pro works on every Mac signed in with your Apple Account (Restora › Restore Purchases), and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
